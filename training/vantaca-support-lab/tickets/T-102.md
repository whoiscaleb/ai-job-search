# T-102 · Balance shows $0.00 but owner hasn't paid October

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Priority | High (money is being misreported) |
| Association | Pine Ridge HOA |
| Unit | 102 (UnitId 2) – owner Tom Becker |
| Reported by | Sandra Ortega, Community Manager |

> Tom Becker in 102 called to ask why his balance shows $0.00. He was honest: he hasn't mailed his October check yet. Our paper ledger says he owes $300. **If balances are wrong for him, they may be wrong for everyone.** Also, unit 104's balance is just blank.

## Your tasks
1. Reproduce with `balance 2` and `balance 4`, and compare against `statement 2` (the statement is correct).
2. Open `dbo.usp_GetUnitBalance` in SSMS (Object Explorer → CommunityLab → Programmability → Stored Procedures → right-click → *Modify*).
3. Debug it by running pieces of the query yourself. Remove the `GROUP BY` and `SUM`s and look at the raw rows the join produces for UnitId 2.
4. Fix the procedure so 102 shows $300.00 and 104 shows $900.00 (not NULL).
5. Check a few other units to confirm your fix didn't break anything (101 should be $0.00).
6. Write the reply to Sandra, and a short note for the knowledge base.
